Local Shorts Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Local Shorts Studio handles information when users edit videos and connect supported social accounts.

2. Information Processed

Depending on the features used, the application may process video files, audio, transcripts, captions, source video URLs, publishing settings, upload history, and account authorization data.

3. Social Account Authorization

Local Shorts Studio supports account authorization through official YouTube and TikTok services.

When authorized, the application may receive access tokens and other account information permitted by the selected authorization scopes. Tokens are used to perform authorized API operations such as video publishing.

4. Data Use

Information is used to:

  • Generate video clips and captions.
  • Process and export edited videos.
  • Manage the video publishing queue.
  • Submit authorized uploads.
  • Record upload status and errors.

5. Local Storage

The desktop application uses local storage for processing and storing application data. This may include generated videos, transcripts, publishing history, and saved authorization data.

Data handling and deletion depend on the installed application version and configuration.

6. Third-Party Services

When users connect or publish to social accounts, relevant information is transmitted to the selected platform through its API.

Each platform processes information according to its own privacy policy.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

TikTok Privacy Policy: TikTok Privacy Policy

7. Security

Users should protect their local computer, API credentials, and account authorization files. Do not share client secrets or authorization tokens publicly.

8. Account Access and Data Deletion

Users may disconnect supported social accounts within the application where that functionality is available.

Users may also revoke application permissions in their Google or TikTok account settings. Revoking authorization does not automatically remove already published videos.

For assistance with locally stored application data, contact the application operator.

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when application features or data-handling practices change.
